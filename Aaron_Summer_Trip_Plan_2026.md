## 🏔️ Uintas Backpacking — 3-Day Trip (Kid-Free Weeks)

You have 8 kid-free windows Jun–Sep. The Uintas are best mid-July through mid-September. Mirror Lake Scenic Byway (UT-150) typically opens by mid-June. No permit required — just a free self-registration at the trailhead.

### ⭐ Best Pick: Jul 7–9 (Mon–Wed)
*Kid-free week: Jul 7–13*

Peak summer weather, snow fully melted, wildflowers out. Leave Monday after exchange. Red Castle or Amethyst Basin both excellent. Back by Wednesday night.

### ⭐ Runner-Up: Aug 18–20 (Mon–Wed)
*Kid-free week: Aug 18–24*

Late summer — fewer crowds than July, still warm, afternoon thunderstorms less frequent. Trails to Naturalist Basin or Christmas Meadows / Amethyst Lake are perfect for 3 days.

### Fall Colors Option: Sep 15–17 (Mon–Wed)
*Kid-free week: Sep 15–21*

Aspens turning gold, very quiet on trails, cool crisp air. Best for solitude. Bring layers — nights will be cold (low 30s°F at elevation).

### Recommended 3-Day Routes

- **Red Castle Lakes Loop** — stunning red-hued peaks, 3 alpine lakes, moderate difficulty. Trailhead: China Meadows.
- **Amethyst Basin via Christmas Meadows** — high alpine lake, gorgeous campsites, moderately challenging. Great for 2 nights in basin.
- **Naturalist Basin** — multiple lakes, well-maintained trail, great for solo backpacking. Trailhead: Highline trail off Mirror Lake Hwy.

> Self-registration permit required at trailhead (free). Mirror Lake Scenic Byway UT-150 open approx. June–October depending on snow.

## 🏖️ Bear Lake — 4-Day Family Trip (With-Kids Weeks)

Bear Lake's turquoise water is stunning and very kid-friendly. July 4th weekend and peak July weekends are extremely crowded — beaches hit capacity by 10am on Saturdays. Best strategy: go mid-week in mid-to-late August or after Labor Day.

### ⭐ Best Pick: Aug 11–14 (Tue–Fri)
*With-kids week: Aug 11–17*

Mid-August hits the sweet spot — warm enough for swimming (lake temps peak), past peak July crowds, most campgrounds still available. Weekdays mean beaches won't cap out. **Book campsites now** — July/August fills up 4 months out.

### ⭐ Runner-Up: Sep 8–11 (Tue–Fri)
*With-kids week: Sep 8–14*

Post-Labor Day is dramatically quieter. Water still swimmable (low 60s°F). Shoulder season = easier reservations, less traffic, peaceful evenings. Highly recommended if kids are okay with slightly cooler water.

### Alternate: Aug 25–28 (Tue–Fri)
*With-kids week: Aug 25–31*

Late August — school starting in many areas thins crowds. Good weather, solid campsite availability.

### Campground Options

- **Rendezvous Beach (Utah side)** — Birch (60 sites), Big Creek (49 sites), Cottonwood (23 sites). Most popular, book via Reserve America.
- **Eastside primitive camps** — Cisco Beach (56 sites), South Eden (20 sites), Rainbow Cove (13 sites, first-come). More rustic, quieter.
- **Bear Lake/Marina KOA** — if you want hookups and amenities.

### Tips

- Go on weekdays — beaches reach capacity by 10am on summer Saturdays.
- Morning beach time before 10am or evening golden hour for best experience.
- Raspberry milkshakes in Garden City are a must.
- Rent kayaks/paddleboards at the marina for a half-day on the water with kids.

> **Reservations:** utahstateparks.reserveamerica.com or 800-322-3770 (Mon–Fri 8am–5pm MT)
